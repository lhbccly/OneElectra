EV Charging Hardware & Global Sourcing

One Electra is the international B2B trading brand of Shenyang Yibu Trading Company (Shenyang, Liaoning, China). We supply certified AC & DC chargers, portable charging piles, charging cables, and adapters to distributors, fleet operators, charge point operators (CPOs), and commercial contractors across Europe, the Middle East, and South Asia.

Core Products & Charging Standards

  • AC Charging Wallboxes (7kW, 11kW, 22kW): Smart home & workplace chargers with Type 1, Type 2, GB/T, and RFID/OCPP 1.6J.
  • DC Fast Chargers (20kW to 360kW+): High-power depot and highway charging piles.
  • Portable EV Chargers (3.5kW, 7kW): Adjustable current travel chargers with multi-plug options.
  • Adapters & Connectors: Type 1, Type 2, GB/T, NACS (Tesla), and DC Type 2 conversion guns.
  • OEM / ODM Private Labeling: Custom enclosure branding, packaging, and custom OCPP firmware integration.
